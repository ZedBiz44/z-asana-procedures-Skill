# Source map

## Operational sources

- [Main Asana guide](https://app.notion.com/p/3dca3e33d5818053a98df9635d006906): the human guide and detailed business examples. Use the hierarchical table of contents.
- [Skill SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3dea3e33d58181bf896dd06bc39818bd): roles, procedure, failure handling, and completion record.
- [Technical source](https://github.com/ZedBiz44/z-asana-procedures-Skill): maintained package, tests, and change history.

The local references are the skill's concise execution instructions derived from the guide, not a full mirrored guide. Keep them aligned when approved procedures change. Exact user instructions and approved scope take precedence over generic examples. Field inventories, available templates, identities, and tool capabilities must be checked live.
